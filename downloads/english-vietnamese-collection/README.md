# English–Vietnamese companion collection

Prepared by Daniel Franjkovic. October 2026 edition.

The complete master and three standalone companions use short English passages followed immediately by corresponding Vietnamese passages. English is deep teal (#174F4B); Vietnamese is warm bronze (#7B4B2A). Headings, tables and learning diagrams follow the same colour key.

The companions map the Fo Guang Shan Hsi Lai Temple primary English scrolls using P1, P2 and P3 section identifiers and original PDF page references. The master includes all three expanded companions, practical orientation, core Buddhist concepts, illustrated Buddhas and bodhisattvas profiles, pronunciation guidance, 389 glossary entries and references. The three complete source liturgies are available through the official links in the repository's main README.

## Editions and interpretation

The supplied Hsi Lai English edition governs the companions. The separate Vietnamese edition attributed to HT. Thích Trí Quang is identified when relevant and is not silently combined with it. New Vietnamese commentary uses respectful standard Buddhist vocabulary familiar to Southern readers. Source quotations, commentary, traditional explanations, reflections and optional applications are distinguished in the books.

Source ambiguities and consequential name differences are explained in editorial notes. In particular, the master does not silently equate the primary English name Prabhūtaratna with the Chinese and secondary Vietnamese Bảo Thắng. Transmitted mantra and Sanskrit sound forms are retained where a reliable translation is not supplied.

New translations and editorial additions were prepared with AI assistance and checked against the supplied sources. They are distinct from the work of the credited source translators, Andrew Nguy and Raymond Kong. These books are independent educational resources and do not claim official temple status or monastic review or endorsement.

## Printing and editing

Use the PDF for predictable printing on A4 at Actual size or 100%. Print a test sheet first to check your printer's margins and colour settings. For double-sided printing, select long-edge binding. The English-first, Vietnamese-beneath order remains useful in greyscale.

The DOCX files contain editable text and tables. Page breaks and contents pagination can change with the fonts, Word version or printer settings on another computer. After editing, update the contents and export a fresh PDF before printing. The supplied PDFs preserve the reviewed layout.
