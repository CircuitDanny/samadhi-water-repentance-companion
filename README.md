# Samādhi Water Repentance Companion

A practical, illustrated companion to all three scrolls of Fo Guang Shan Hsi Lai Temple's *Liturgy of the Samādhi Water Repentance of Kindness and Compassion*, available in English and an English-Vietnamese master edition.

The collection explains what each major section is doing, why it is chanted, which Buddhist ideas it assumes and how its Buddhas and bodhisattvas relate to the practice. The September 2026 expansion adds closer reading notes, everyday examples and fuller figure profiles while preserving the established artwork and visual style.

### Recommended order

Begin with the master edition's new **Beginning a Buddhist practice** section for the Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path, Five Precepts, refuge and a simple daily routine. Then read the three practice companions alongside their matching official scrolls. Keep the illustrated figure guide and glossary nearby for quick reference.

### English-Vietnamese Edition

[Download the complete English-Vietnamese master](downloads/04-master-english-vietnamese-edition.pdf). The 232-page edition contains 116 matched page pairs. Each English page is immediately followed by the corresponding Vietnamese page. Both languages retain the same complete content, illustrations and reference material, with translated diagrams and matching page-pair numbers.

The Vietnamese uses standard spelling and Buddhist vocabulary familiar to Southern readers. It translates the companion commentary, not a Vietnamese temple's liturgy. Sanskrit and source chanting forms are retained; English pronunciation cues are labelled as such. The edition was prepared with AI assistance and has not yet been reviewed by a Vietnamese-speaking Buddhist teacher. Teacher and reader corrections are welcome.

## What this guide adds

- section-by-section explanations, attention cues and transitions for Scrolls One, Two and Three;
- source-page notes on difficult English, apparent typographical errors and unresolved terms;
- worked everyday examples connecting confession, restraint, repair and renewed vows;
- clear accounts of karma, repentance, rebirth, the six realms, emptiness, Pure Land and merit;
- a full explanation of the six senses;
- expanded figure profiles with pictures, pronunciation, stories, relationships, recognition cues, source trails and practice pathways;
- diagrams for the ritual sequence and difficult doctrines;
- a visual map of the ten directions and a concise figure-role chart;
- careful treatment of uncertain history and compressed ritual terms;
- a complete Vietnamese translation in the bilingual master, with matching page pairs and translated diagrams.

## Editing and rebuilding

The component Markdown files generate the six English PDFs and combined master Markdown. The bilingual builder measures matching English and Vietnamese blocks together before choosing page breaks, so translation cannot drift to a different page pair. See [BUILDING.md](BUILDING.md) for commands, font requirements and verification steps.

The [scroll revision evidence](source-notes/SCROLL_REVISION_EVIDENCE.md) and [figure revision evidence](source-notes/REFERENCE_REVISION_EVIDENCE.md) record source checks and editorial decisions. The original liturgies remain the authority for the text being chanted.

## Scope

The Hsi Lai English translation is the main source. Chinese and Mandarin pinyin were consulted when an English term was compressed or unclear. The source does not contain Vietnamese. The bilingual edition translates this independent companion, while retaining references to the original source PDFs. Temple chanting should follow the officiant and the temple's own transmitted text.

The companion explains a Chinese Mahāyāna liturgy as practised in a Fo Guang Shan context. It does not claim that every Buddhist tradition uses the same terminology or interpretation.
