# Evidence for the expanded figure profiles

Checked 27 September 2026. This note records the new evidence used in `FIGURES.md`, the targeted reading aids and the revised references. It does not replace the three existing source-page maps or claim that all older links were rechecked.

## Scope and editorial rules

- The official Fo Guang Shan Hsi Lai Temple English PDFs remain the controlling source for where and how a figure appears in the Water Repentance.
- Canonical or traditional stories are labelled. Museum records support visual recognition and relationship notes, not doctrine by themselves.
- Recognition attributes are presented as cues with regional limits. No new portrait or biography is assigned to Mahāvyūha, the long litany names or collective bodhisattva invocations.
- The expansion concerns Buddhas, bodhisattvas and terms required to read those profiles. It does not attempt a general history of Buddhism.

## Remaining limits

- Mahāvyūha remains a securely attested litany name but not a securely identified independent narrative or portrait in the sources checked.
- The five long Buddha names in the repeated litany retain cautious name-element explanations only.
- Art attributes identify a type within a tradition. An unlabelled image should be checked against inscription, provenance, ensemble and collection record before being named.
- The official English source's unusual wording remains the controlling liturgical wording. The profiles' translation notes are explanatory aids rather than proposed corrections.
