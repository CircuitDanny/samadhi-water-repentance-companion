# Bilingual visual review

The final 232-page master was rendered on 27 September 2026. All page contact sheets were inspected, with representative full-page checks of the front matter, practice guidance, scrolls, dense tables, mantra boxes, diagrams, figure profiles and glossary.

The review found no overlapping or clipped text, broken table cells, missing Vietnamese glyphs or unintended blank pages. Section dividers are intentional. Matching English and Vietnamese table rows remain paired. Profile titles and recognition cues were kept with their artwork after review identified awkward page breaks.

A few short continuation pages retain extra white space to preserve readable type and exact content pairing. The edition deliberately allows both languages room to flow, rather than compressing the longer language.

The automated report is downloads/bilingual-validation.json. It verifies all 2,646 source text blocks in each language, 514 annotations and 274 outline destinations, with no reported errors. All 116 page pairs are present, including cover, edition note and contents pairs.

The 110-page English master was also checked and rendered. Its new practice section was visually inspected; all 96 retained body pages after that insertion match the previously checked expanded edition's text apart from page numbering.
