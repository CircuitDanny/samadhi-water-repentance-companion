# Vietnamese reference translation review

Completed 27 September 2026.

All six reference chapters are translated: MASTER_OVERVIEW, CONCEPTS, FIGURES, PRONUNCIATION, GLOSSARY and REFERENCES. The 389 glossary entries retain their English lookup terms beside the Vietnamese terms, in the original English alphabetical order. The complete GLOSSARY.md is the canonical build source; glossary-parts preserves the two reviewed editorial batches used to assemble it.

Markdown block structure, heading levels, paragraph and list-item counts, table-cell counts, image paths, URLs and source references match the English chapters. Sanskrit and mantra forms are retained. English pronunciation approximations are explicitly identified as English sound cues. Uncertain interpretations remain labelled as uncertain.

Vietnamese uses standard spelling and accessible Southern Buddhist vocabulary, including sanh, chánh and tánh không. Figure terminology, the distinction between Pure Lands and deva realms, and compressed glossary terms received an additional editorial pass. The starting-practice chapter was independently reviewed for meaning and natural phrasing.

The final PDF verification checks every source text block against the corresponding language pages, matched page-unit identities, source hashes, bookmarks, internal links, extracted glyphs and page bounds. See downloads/bilingual-validation.json for the final build result.

The translation is AI-assisted and has not been approved by a Vietnamese-speaking Buddhist teacher. It translates the companion commentary; it does not establish a Vietnamese chanting text for the Hsi Lai liturgy.
